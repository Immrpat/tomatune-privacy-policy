Privacy Policy

Tomatune is an Android focus timer. Your focus history, tasks, intentions, and notes stay in the app on your device. Tomatune does not send them to the developer or to a server.

Last updated October 14, 2026
🔒

The short version: Tomatune has no account, analytics, or crash-reporting service. It is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses the Internet. Your focus history, tasks, and notes stay on your device and are never sent to the ad service.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Tomatune Android app (package com.tomatune.app), maintained by @Immrpat. It describes the app version reviewed on the date above. It does not cover Android, your device maker, Google Play, or other services under their own control.

Information stored on your device

Tomatune stores the information needed to run and personalize the timer:

  • Focus history: session phase and times, planned and elapsed minutes, completion status, sound choice, and any task or intention attached to a focus session.
  • Tasks and parked thoughts: task titles, estimates and completion state; note text, creation time, and whether a note is marked done. Text you enter in a task, intention, or note stays in app storage.
  • Settings and progress: timer lengths, sound and volume preferences, daily goal, selected looks, and progress or achievement state.
  • Timer recovery state: while a session is active, the app saves enough local state to restore it if Android stops the app process.

How the app uses it

This information is used on your device to run focus and break timers, play audio bundled with the app, show notifications, associate sessions with tasks, save notes, and calculate your history and progress. Tomatune does not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Information sent off your device

Tomatune does not send your focus history, tasks, intentions, notes, or settings to the developer or to any service. The app has no sign-in, cloud sync, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

The only network use is advertising, described below.

Tomatune can show Android notifications for the timer when you allow notifications. Android controls that permission and displays the notification. The app uses Android's foreground media playback and wake-lock facilities so a session can continue while the screen is off. These features do not upload your session data.

Advertising

Tomatune shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): a full-screen ad may appear when a break starts, and you can choose to watch a video ad to unlock a Pro sound for 24 hours. Ads never appear during a focus session.

According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • App interactions with ads, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about ad performance.
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is handled by Google under How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer does not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, Tomatune asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Privacy → Ads). "Delete all data" in Tomatune removes data stored in the app; it cannot delete data held by Google.

Storage, security, and backups

App data is kept in Android's private storage for Tomatune. The app does not operate a server containing your data. The app excludes its data from Android cloud backup. Phone-to-phone migration behavior can vary by Android version and device maker.

Keep your device and its screen lock secure. Anyone who can access your unlocked device and open Tomatune may be able to see data stored in the app.

Retention and deletion

Your local data remains on the device until you delete it or remove the app. In Tomatune, open Settings → Delete all data to remove session history, tasks, parked thoughts, saved timer state, and progress. This also ends a running focus session. Timer settings are kept. You can also clear Tomatune's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Tomatune has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete and no server-side account data to request.

Hosting of this policy page

That logging is performed by GitHub as the host, not by the Tomatune app. Read GitHub's General Privacy Statement for details. This page contains no analytics scripts or embedded third-party media.

Changes and questions

If Tomatune's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change. For privacy questions, use the repository's Issues page.
